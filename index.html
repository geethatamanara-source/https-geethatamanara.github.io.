About me

The analyst in the middle

I sit between the people who run a business and the teams who build its systems. I'm happiest in a workshop room working out what the real problem is, then turning it into requirements an engineering team can build from.

Most of my work is in CRM transformation: evaluating platforms, mapping data flows and integrations, and redesigning the processes around them. I also go deep on the data, with SQL segmentation that lifted customer conversion by 18%.
